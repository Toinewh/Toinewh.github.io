Contact’Up — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Contact’Up is an app for iPhone and iPad that helps you tidy the contacts on your device. It is developed by Antoine Bunnens.

Contact’Up collects no data. It has no account and no server, and nothing it reads is sent to the developer.

Your contacts

Contact’Up reads your contacts through Apple’s Contacts framework, with the access you grant in iOS, and writes to them only when you apply the changes you have prepared. Your contacts stay on your device and in the accounts they already belong to, such as iCloud. Contact’Up sends them nowhere.

What the app keeps on your device

To show what was changed and to let you undo it, Contact’Up keeps on your device:

  • the history of the changes it made, and of the changes it noticed were made outside the app;
  • a copy of the text values of your contacts, without their photos, used to notice what changed elsewhere;
  • the changes you have prepared and not yet applied;
  • the suggestions you turned down;
  • the answers Apple Maps gave about addresses;
  • your preferences.

The copy of your contacts is excluded from iCloud and computer backups and is protected by iOS while the device is locked. The rest is part of your device’s backups, like the data of any app. The history, its copy of your contacts, the Maps answers and the suggestions you turned down can each be cleared from within the app. Deleting the app removes everything it kept.

Apple Maps

When Contact’Up checks an address — when a card is opened, or when you ask it to check the addresses of your address book — the address alone is sent to Apple’s geocoding service, to learn how Apple Maps writes it. No name or other detail of the contact is sent with it. The request is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive it.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, Contact’Up may ask Apple Intelligence to help read a line you type when creating a contact. This runs on the device.

No tracking, advertising or analytics

Contact’Up contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code, and does not track you. If you have chosen, in the settings of your device, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
