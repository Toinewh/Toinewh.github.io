Contact’Up — Support

Contact’Up helps you tidy the contacts on your iPhone and iPad: it imports CSV, spreadsheet and vCard files, finds duplicates, mistakes and missing details, and proposes a fix for each. Nothing is written to your contacts until you apply the changes you prepared, and every change can be undone.

Contact’Up does not see all my contacts

Contact’Up works with the access you granted it. To give it all your contacts, open Settings on your device, then Apps, Contact’Up, Contacts, and choose full access. With limited access, it works with the contacts you shared with it only.

How do I undo a change?

Every change is recorded in the History tab. Open a session to undo it whole, or open a contact to undo some of its fields only.

A suggestion I turned down no longer appears

A suggestion you turn down is not asked again. To bring one back, open Clean Up, then its menu, then Ignored proposals.

Why are addresses checked with Apple Maps?

To propose how an address is usually written. Only the address is sent to Apple, with no name or other detail of the contact. The answers are kept on your device; Forget verified addresses, in the Clean Up menu, removes them.

Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Please describe what you did and what you saw, and leave out names, numbers and addresses from your contacts: the issue is public.

Privacy

Contact’Up collects no data. See the privacy policy.
